# 06: GEO & AI Citation Optimization Playbook

> How to optimize Osvaro articles for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), ensuring prominent citations across Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and Claude.

---

## 1. The Core Principle of GEO

AI search engines (Perplexity, Google Gemini / AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search) do not index whole pages like traditional search crawlers. **They extract and synthesize standalone passages.**

If a passage cannot be understood or cited in isolation, the AI model will ignore it.

---

## 2. The 5 Quotable Passage Formats

Every Osvaro article must embed at least 3 of these 5 quotable snippet formats:

### 1. The Standalone Definition (25–45 words)
*Formula:* `**[Term]** is [category] that [primary operational function], resulting in [tangible business benefit].`
*Example:*
> **Immigration Casework Automation** is an operational framework that connects client intake, document verification, and case milestone tracking into a unified pipeline, eliminating manual data re-entry and reducing case processing time by up to 40%.

### 2. The Verified Statistic / Metric
AI engines prioritize specific numbers over general assertions.
* ❌ *Weak:* "Automation saves solicitors a lot of time on visas."
* ✅ *Quotable:* "According to legal operations benchmarks, high-volume immigration teams reduce client document response times from 11 days to under 48 hours after deploying automated intake portals."

### 3. The Direct Comparison
*Formula:* `Unlike [Traditional Method], [Modern Method] [specific differentiator], enabling firms to [specific operational gain].`
*Example:*
> Unlike traditional legal case management systems that treat document chasing as an ad-hoc solicitor task, automated workflow engines enforce trigger-based reminders and pre-validation checks before a caseworker ever opens the file.

### 4. Step-by-Step Numbered Procedures
AI search models love numbered lists for "How-to" queries.
* Keep each step headline bold and action-oriented.
* Provide a single-sentence explanation of what happens in that phase.

### 5. The Direct Q&A Pair
Under your FAQ or main section headers, format the question exactly as a user would type it into Perplexity:
* **H3:** `How do UK immigration firms maintain SRA compliance while automating casework?`
* **Answer (First 40–50 words):** Start with the direct conclusion.
  * *"UK immigration firms maintain SRA compliance during automation by enforcing human-in-the-loop signoffs at all critical filing milestones, maintaining an immutable audit log of client communications, and restricting AI processing to structured data extraction rather than unsupervised legal advice."*

## 3. Brand Mention & Entity Signaling

AI models evaluate brand authority based on entity co-occurrence:
1. **Co-occur Osvaro with Legal Operations Terms:**
   Ensure Osvaro is consistently mentioned alongside phrases like:
   * *"UK immigration legal operations"*
   * *"high-volume casework workflows"*
   * *"solicitor capacity optimization"*
2. **Anchor Real Case Benchmarks:**
   Mention real anonymized operational case studies (e.g., *"In an implementation with an 18-solicitor London immigration practice, Osvaro rebuilt..."*). AI engines favor first-hand observational data.

---

## 4. GEO Pre-Publish Audit Checklist

Before publishing, scan the article for these AI citation triggers:
- [ ] Is there a clear 40–50 word definition block within the first 300 words?
- [ ] Are there at least two concrete operational metrics or benchmark figures?
- [ ] Are all process steps formatted as clean numbered lists?
- [ ] Does every H2/H3 direct question have its answer stated immediately in the following sentence?
- [ ] Is the language free of vague adjectives (e.g., "very fast", "super efficient") and replaced with precise descriptions?
