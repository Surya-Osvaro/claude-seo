# 01: Keyword Research & Search Intent Guide

> Master playbook for discovering, prioritizing, and mapping keywords for Osvaro blog content targeting UK immigration law firms and legal operations.

---

## 1. The 8-Phase Research Workflow

When planning content for a new topic or cluster, follow these 8 phases:

### Phase 1: Scope & Goal Definition
Define the boundary of the keyword search:
* **Target Audience:** Managing Partners, Senior Immigration Solicitors, Heads of Operations, Practice Managers at high-volume UK immigration firms.
* **Core Pain Points:** Casework bottlenecks, SRA/Home Office compliance risks, client document chasing delays, fee-earner burnout, manual data entry in case management systems.
* **Geography:** UK (`en-GB`).
* **Search Context:** B2B commercial intent + high-authority informational intent.

---

### Phase 2: Seed Discovery & Striking Distance
* **Striking-Distance Queries:** First look at terms your site or existing pages already rank for on positions 5–20. These are fast wins.
* **Seed Buckets:**
  * *Problem-focused:* "immigration casework bottlenecks", "solicitor manual data entry", "OISC compliance audit prep"
  * *Solution-focused:* "immigration case management automation", "client onboarding portal for law firms"
  * *Operational metrics:* "cost per immigration case", "casework velocity legaltech"

---

### Phase 3: Expansion & Modifiers
Use expansion patterns to find high-intent, long-tail search terms:

```markdown
### High-Intent B2B Modifiers
- Best [keyword] for UK law firms
- How to automate [process] in immigration law
- [Problem] solutions for high-volume law firms
- [Tool A] vs [Tool B] for immigration casework
- Step-by-step guide to [compliance / workflow]
- [Keyword] checklist / template / workflow
```

---

### Phase 4: Intent Classification

| Intent | Intent Signals | Typical Query | Osvaro Target Content Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Informational** | how to, what is, guide, rules, requirements | "how to streamline spouse visa casework" | In-depth operational guides, workflow breakdowns |
| **Commercial** | best, comparison, vs, software, tools, review | "best immigration law case management software UK" | Comparison guides, feature scorecards, buyer guides |
| **Transactional** | demo, pricing, hire, implementation, agency | "law firm workflow automation demo" | Product pages, case study walkthroughs, booking CTA |

---

### Phase 5: Scoring & Prioritization

Use the B2B Opportunity Formula:
$$\text{Opportunity Score} = \frac{\text{Search Volume} \times \text{Intent Multiplier}}{\text{Keyword Difficulty (KD)}}$$

* **Intent Multipliers:**
  * Informational = 1.0
  * Commercial Investigation = 2.5
  * High-Intent Transactional = 3.5

**Priority Matrix:**
* **Quick Wins (Priority 1):** Low KD (<30), High Commercial Intent, Low-to-Medium Volume (200–800/mo). *In B2B legaltech, 200 monthly searches of managing partners are worth 100x more than 10,000 consumer searches.*
* **Pillar Topics (Priority 2):** Medium-High Volume, Medium KD (30–60), broad informational guide.
* **Long-Term Authority (Priority 3):** High KD (>60) competitive head terms.

---

### Phase 6: GEO / AI-Answer Readiness Check
Flag whether this query triggers an AI Overview (Google AIO, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search):
* Does the query ask a direct question ("How does...", "What is the benchmark for...")?
* Can it be summarized in a 40–60 word snippet?
* If yes, the blog post **must** include a dedicated snippet answer block directly under the H2.

---

### Phase 7: Semantic Grouping & Clustering
Never target single isolated keywords. Group them into a **Hub & Spoke Cluster**:
* **Pillar Keyword (Hub):** e.g., "UK Immigration Law Firm Automation"
* **Spoke 1:** "Automating Sponsor Licence Compliance Audits"
* **Spoke 2:** "Reducing Chasing Time for Visa Supporting Documents"
* **Spoke 3:** "Fee-Earner Capacity Scaling Without New Hires"

---

### Phase 8: Deliverable Output Shape

Before drafting any post, output a structured Keyword Brief:
1. **Target Primary Keyword** (+ Search Volume & Intent)
2. **Top 3–5 Secondary Keywords** (Long-tail variations to naturally weave into H2s/H3s)
3. **People Also Ask (PAA) Questions** (To include in the FAQ section)
4. **Primary Competitor URL currently ranking #1**
5. **Expected Searcher Intent & Next Action**
