> The quality gate ensuring Osvaro content passes Google's Helpful Content System, satisfies YMYL/legal compliance thresholds, and establishes genuine industry authority.

## 1. Google's "Who / How / Why" Canonical Test

Every Osvaro article must pass Google’s core three-question heuristic before publication:

| Question | What Google & Readers Look For | How Osvaro Satisfies It |
|---|---|---|
| **Who created it?** | Visible byline, professional credentials, lived experience in the industry. | Articles attributed to Osvaro's operations leads, with a clear author bio highlighting legaltech & casework engineering expertise. |
| **How was it created?** | Transparent process, first-hand evidence, original data, practical problem-solving. | Content grounded in real operational implementations, showing actual workflow blueprints and tested system architectures. |
| **Why does it exist?** | Created primarily to solve a real human problem, NOT just to game search rankings. | The guide provides actionable operational systems that managing partners can implement regardless of whether they hire Osvaro. |

## 2. E-E-A-T Framework for Legaltech Operations

Because Osvaro speaks to solicitors and operates adjacent to legal services, Google evaluates content with high scrutiny:

### Experience (First-Hand Operational Knowledge)
* Include real-world operational scenarios (e.g., handling Sponsor Management System submissions during peak season).
* Show screenshots, workflow diagrams, or system logic tables that prove actual field experience.

### Expertise
* Accurate use of legal and operational terminology (e.g., SRA Standards & Regulations, OISC regulations, Home Office defined CoS allocations, fee-earner utilization rates).
* Zero technical inaccuracies regarding UK immigration categories (Skilled Worker, Sponsor Licences, Global Talent).

### Authoritativeness
* Cite primary official sources where relevant (e.g., Gov.uk Home Office sponsor guidance, SRA transparency rules, Law Society operational reports).
* Provide unique operational points of view that demonstrate industry leadership.

### Trustworthiness
* Clean editorial corrections, clear contact info, physical presence, transparent disclosures.
* Zero exaggerated marketing claims or guarantees regarding Home Office visa approvals.

## 3. Strict Anti-Hallucination & Legal Compliance Rules

When AI writes or assists in drafting:

1. **Zero Fabricated Regulations:**
   Never invent Home Office rules, visa fees, salary thresholds, or SRA codes. If citing a specific UK policy rule (e.g., Skilled Worker minimum salary threshold), verify it or mark it `[verify current Home Office guidance]`.
2. **Never Offer Direct Legal Advice:**
   Osvaro provides **operational systems and workflow engineering for law practices**, not legal advice to visa applicants. Maintain this clear positioning throughout all articles.
3. **No Phantom Statistics:**
   Never cite an unverified study or fake research paper. If using an operational benchmark, frame it as an observed industry metric or cite the recognized source (e.g., *Legal Trends Report*, *SRA Risk Outlook*).

## 4. The Pre-Publish Editorial Gate (Score /10)

Before approving any draft for publishing on Osvaro's blog:
- [ ] **Author Bio & Byline:** Present and verified.
- [ ] **Who/How/Why Passed:** The article clearly states how these systems were tested.
- [ ] **No Fluff Words:** Zero generic AI filler phrases ("revolutionize", "game changer").
- [ ] **No Legal Advice Disclaimer:** If discussing compliance, framed strictly around operational workflows and audit trail preservation.
- [ ] **Fact Check Completed:** Every Home Office reference corresponds to current UK policy.
