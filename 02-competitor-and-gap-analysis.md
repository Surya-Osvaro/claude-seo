# 02: Competitor & Content Gap Analysis Playbook

> How to evaluate existing top-ranking competitor articles, uncover their blind spots, and craft superior content for Osvaro.

---

## 1. The 3 Gap Types to Identify

When auditing top 5 ranking competitors for any immigration ops keyword:

### 1. Topic Gaps (Missing Subtopics)
* What critical aspects did the competitor fail to mention?
* *Example:* A competitor writes about "Immigration Case Management Systems" but ignores **Home Office Sponsor Management System (SMS) API sync** or **GDPR/SRA client file retention compliance**.
* *Action:* Make this missing subtopic a primary H2 in Osvaro's article.

### 2. Depth Gaps (Surface-Level / Fluff)
* Did the competitor just provide generic bullet points without operational steps?
* *Example:* Competitor says "automate client reminders", but doesn't explain *how* (trigger-based WhatsApp/SMS vs automated email sequences vs client portal upload reminders).
* *Action:* Provide exact workflows, timeline diagrams, and concrete operational numbers.

### 3. Authority & Proof Gaps (Lack of First-Hand Evidence)
* Did the competitor use generic AI-generated copy with zero real quotes or case benchmarks?
* *Action:* Anchor Osvaro's post with tangible operational realities (e.g., "A 15-solicitor immigration practice spends an average of 14 hours per week purely reconciling client visa documents across Outlook and Dropbox").

---

## 2. SERP Competitor Triage Protocol

When evaluating the SERP for a target query:

1. **Filter Out Non-Competitors:**
   Do not benchmark against Wikipedia, Gov.uk, Reddit, or general job boards. Gov.uk provides legal definitions; Osvaro provides **operational systems to handle those requirements efficiently**.
2. **Benchmark Against Commercial & Agency Competitors:**
   Look at legal software vendors (Clio, Actionstep, LEAP, Digitory Legal) and legal consultancies.
3. **Score the Top 3 Competitors (1–10 Scale):**
   * **Depth:** How detailed is their operational advice?
   * **Readability:** Are there tables, callouts, and clear bullet points?
   * **GEO/Citability:** Do they offer quotable definitions and benchmark figures?
   * **Outdatedness:** Is their guidance citing 2022/2023 immigration rules instead of current Home Office operational standards?

---

## 3. Gap Prioritization Formula

Prioritize which gaps to attack using this scoring matrix:

$$\text{Gap Priority} = \frac{\text{Business Impact} \times \text{Competitive Advantage}}{\text{Effort}}$$

* **High Priority (Attack Immediately):** Topics where competitors have thin/outdated content, search intent is commercial, and Osvaro has a strong, unique operational solution.
* **Medium Priority (Strategic Build):** Comprehensive guides where competitors are strong, requiring Osvaro to produce a definitive, long-form pillar piece with original data.
* **Low Priority:** Generic informational queries with low commercial intent and entrenched high-DR competitors.

---

## 4. The "Beat the Competitor" Checklist

Before finalizing any Osvaro draft, verify:
- [ ] We answered the user's primary query in the first 100 words (Competitors often bury the lead).
- [ ] We included at least one comparison table or operational flowchart that competitors lack.
- [ ] We addressed modern AI/automation workflows that 2+ year old competitor posts ignore.
- [ ] We provided concrete metrics (e.g., hours saved, reduction in case error rate).
