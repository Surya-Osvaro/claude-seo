# 04: Content Brief Generator Blueprint

> The operational blueprint for generating rigorous, research-backed content briefs before writing a single word of copy.

---

## 1. Why Every Article Starts with a Brief

Writing without a brief leads to generic, meandering AI content. A brief locks in:
1. Exact search intent and reader persona.
2. Mandatory subtopics and competitive gaps.
3. Keyword distribution across headers.
4. GEO/AI citation snippet targets.
5. Internal linking and CTA pathways.

---

## 2. Standard Osvaro Content Brief Template

When planning any post, generate this exact brief structure:

```markdown
# Content Brief: [Working Title]

## 1. Core Metadata
- **Target Persona:** [e.g., Managing Partner / Head of Operations at high-volume UK immigration firm]
- **Search Intent:** [Informational / Commercial Investigation / Problem-Aware]
- **Primary Keyword:** [Keyword] (Vol: [X], KD: [Y])
- **Secondary Keywords:** [Keyword 1], [Keyword 2], [Keyword 3], [Keyword 4]
- **Recommended Word Count:** [e.g., 1,500 – 2,200 words]
- **Target URL Slug:** `/blog/[clean-descriptive-slug]`

## 2. Competitive Angle & Differentiator
- **Top Competitor:** [URL of #1 ranking article]
- **Competitor Weakness:** [Why current ranking content is weak: e.g., outdated 2023 rules, surface-level generic advice, zero operational data]
- **Osvaro's "Unfair Advantage":** [Specific operational insight, system blueprint, or practical metric we provide that nobody else does]

## 3. Detailed Outline & Word Count Target

### [H1] [Primary Headline with Primary Keyword] (~60 chars)
* **Goal:** Hook the reader immediately with an operational truth.
* **Intro (~150 words):** Address the core bottleneck immediately. Pass the "Who/How/Why" test in the opening paragraph.

### [H2] [Core Definition / Direct Answer Block] (~250 words)
* **GEO Snippet Target:** 40–60 word direct, quotable summary directly beneath the heading.
* **Key Concept:** Explain the operational concept simply.

### [H2] [The Breakdown: The Operational Bottleneck] (~400 words)
* **Secondary Keyword Included:** [Keyword 1]
* **Format:** Bulleted list or comparison of traditional vs automated approach.
* **Evidence:** Real operational metrics (e.g., time wasted, error rates).

### [H2] [The Framework / Step-by-Step Blueprint] (~600 words)
* **Secondary Keyword Included:** [Keyword 2]
* **Steps:** Step 1, Step 2, Step 3 with practical execution rules.
* **Visual Aid:** Callout box or markdown flowchart.

### [H2] [Common Traps & Compliance Considerations] (~300 words)
* **Secondary Keyword Included:** [Keyword 3]
* **Focus:** SRA / Home Office compliance boundaries, avoiding client churn.

### [H2] [Frequently Asked Questions] (~300 words)
* **PAA Question 1:** [Direct 45-word answer]
* **PAA Question 2:** [Direct 45-word answer]
* **PAA Question 3:** [Direct 45-word answer]

### [H2] [Conclusion & Next Operational Step] (~150 words)
* **Summary:** Wrap up with practical action item.
* **CTA:** Contextual link to Osvaro operational audit or consultation.

## 4. Internal Link Targets
- **Parent Pillar Page:** [Title + Target URL]
- **Related Blog Spoke 1:** [Title + Anchor phrase]
- **Conversion Destination:** [Osvaro audit booking page]
```

---

## 3. Quality Gate Before Writing
Confirm the brief satisfies:
- [ ] Primary keyword is in the H1 and at least one H2.
- [ ] At least one dedicated GEO snippet target is explicitly assigned.
- [ ] Tone aligns with senior legal professionals (respectful, rigorous, no hype).
